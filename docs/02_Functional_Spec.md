**Language note.** The product UI is in Indonesian. Text quoted from the UI is kept in Indonesian and followed by an English gloss where it helps. Role names in the UI: Owner, Admin Gudang (Warehouse Admin), Viewer. Enum values and codes (BL, S, P, E) are language-neutral.

## How to use this document

- **Rule priority:** the section "Design decisions in effect" (cross-feature product rules) overrides the Business logic (BL) table, and both override the screen and popup lists. If two sections conflict, follow the one higher up, then fix the one that lost.
- **Reference codes:** S = screen, P = popup, BL = business logic, E = entity (see `03_Data_Model.md`).
- **"This section" or "this document"** inside text that was moved over from the old PRD means a section in this document.
- **Rules not yet merged:** some product rules still live in the "Design decisions in effect" table and have not been merged into the matching BL row. Gradually, when touching a BL item, merge the related rules into that BL row and delete the duplicates.

## Roles and permissions

There are four roles. Permissions are checked on the server on every action, not only by hiding buttons in the UI.

| Role            | Who                                    | Allowed                                                                                                                                                  | Not allowed                                               |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform admin  | The product owner (platform operator)  | See the list of organizations, subscription status, invoices, and webhook events (without stock contents); change an organization's status with a reason | See or change an organization's stock data                |
| Owner           | The owner of the customer company      | Everything in their organization: warehouses, products, members, subscription                                                                            | See other organizations                                   |
| Warehouse Admin | The person responsible for a warehouse | Receive, adjust, and transfer stock in the assigned warehouses                                                                                           | Manage products, warehouses, members, or the subscription |
| Viewer          | An auditor or manager                  | Read stock and history in the assigned warehouses                                                                                                        | Change any data                                           |

Additional rule: a transfer from warehouse A to B may only be made by a user who has rights to warehouse A (the source). The destination warehouse may be any active warehouse of the organization, including one not assigned to that user. The recipient only needs to be visible in warehouse B's history.

| Trial                          | The trial lasts 30 days from sign-up with the same limits as Starter (2 warehouses, 100 products, 3 members). This check is only done when choosing and is not repeated on payment: while a downgrade invoice is Pending, the organization's effective limit is the smaller of the current plan and the target plan, so usage cannot rise past the target plan's limits before payment. The effective limit is released when the invoice becomes Paid, Expired, or Failed, or when the Owner chooses "Batalkan turun paket" (cancel downgrade) in S16 (the invoice becomes Expired). Period access ends at 23:59 WIB on the end date, and the due date equals the end of the current period. There are four invoice kinds with different validity: initial (created by the Owner from a trial, valid 7 days from creation), plan change (created by the Owner, 7 days), regular renewal (created by the system 7 days before the end of the period, valid until due date + 10 days), and replacement renewal (created by the Owner through "Bayar tagihan" (pay the bill) after the regular invoice has expired, valid for the longer of due date + 10 days and creation date + 7 days). **Stock rules that must be written in the code and the README**

- Stock must not be negative, guarded in the application **and** the database.
- History is never changed or deleted; corrections are made as new rows.
- A transfer to the same warehouse is rejected. The quantity must be a positive integer, at most 1,000,000 per row.
- A transfer completes immediately; there is no "in transit" status.

**Important flow notes**

- **Transfer:** S06 or S09 → S11 → P11 (confirmation) → S12 → S10. Every stock write ends as a row in StockMovement that can be opened from P13.
- **Payment:** S16 → P16 → the Xendit page → S22. The final status is determined by the webhook (BL22), not by the page the user visits on returning. The initial and plan-change flow: S16 → P15 → P16 → the Xendit page → S22.
- **Suspension:** BL23 is computed on every request; if SUSPENDED, every tenant screen redirects to S21 except the Owner's S16 and S22.
- **A platform admin** only sees S19 and S20; it has no access to S04 through S16 because it has no organization.
- **Password reset:** S14 (Buat tautan reset) → P08 → the member opens `/reset/[token]` → S01. Its view has not been designed.
